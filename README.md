# Boid Sim
It's a simulation of how Simple rules such as:

 - Separation: Steers away from nearby boids that are too close to avoid crowding and collisions.
 - Alignment: Steers toward the average heading or velocity of nearby boids so the group moves in a similar direction.
 - Cohesion: Steers toward the average position (centre of mass) of nearby boids to keep the flock together

To create a flock-like behaviour



## Tech-Stack
 - Cpp
 - Shader(Fragment, for rendering)

 - And use the up and down arrow keys to increase or decrease its speed
